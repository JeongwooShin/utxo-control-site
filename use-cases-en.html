When do you need UTXO Control?

When which deposit you spend matters as much as how much you send.

01 · Spend interest, leave principal outputs untouched

One anticipated use is receiving Consol interest after BTCMobick’s public transition. If interest arrives as separate, spendable UTXOs, you could select those deposits without spending the principal outputs.

This is a conditional example, not a confirmed payout policy.

Transition dates, Consol payout arrangements, eligibility and principal-retention rules require separate official confirmation. The app guarantees neither interest nor eligibility. If principal and interest have already been combined into one output, sending only the interest amount still consumes that original output.

02 · Choose particular mining rewards

If mining proceeds are held in separate spendable outputs, coin selection can help move only the chosen reward UTXOs. A mining-pool payout is not necessarily a direct coinbase reward.

Direct mining rewards may have maturity requirements before spending. Verify BTCMobick’s own network rules; do not assume Bitcoin’s block count applies.

Not a Freshcoin certification or preservation tool.

Sending consumes the selected reward output and creates new outputs. The app does not determine or guarantee whether a transferred output qualifies as “Freshcoin” or retains any associated value or eligibility.

03 · Keep specific deposits unspent

Leave the deposits you want to preserve unselected and use other outputs. Review the selection, destination, amount and fee before sending. Combining outputs may reveal a relationship between them on-chain.

Before sending

  • Match the TXID and output index with your deposit records.
  • Do not select outputs you want to preserve.
  • Check confirmation, spendability and fees.
  • Use private keys only in the app, never on this website.

References: Bitcoin Developer Guide — Transactions · Block Chain · Official Mobick website
