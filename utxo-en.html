What is a UTXO?

One balance. Several individual outputs you can choose to spend.

An output that has not been spent

UTXO means Unspent Transaction Output: an output created by a transaction that has not yet been spent. A single address can hold several UTXOs.

One addressA 10 + B 2 + C 3 = total 15
Select only BB is used in the transaction.
A and C remain unspent by this transaction.

Selecting an output is not choosing an amount

A selected UTXO is consumed in full. Sending less creates a recipient output and usually a new change output. The difference between total inputs and outputs is the fee.

A partial payment does not preserve the original output.

The original UTXO is spent and new outputs are created. To preserve a particular original output, leave it unselected.

What can you choose in the app?

UTXO Control lets BTCMobick users choose outputs by amount and transaction reference (TXID and output index). It does not automatically identify interest, principal or Freshcoin. Compare the entries with your deposit records.

Your selection must also cover the fee. MAX deducts the fee from the selected total. Legacy and Native SegWit outputs cannot be combined in a single transaction in this app.
